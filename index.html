🎭 The Heart of a Storyteller

I have always believed that life speaks in whispers—in the subtle flicker of an actor's eye before they deliver a line, in the golden hour light spilling across an empty street, in the resonant crack of a shuttlecock, or in the silent logic of an algorithm waiting to compile.

Whether I am on stage immersing myself into a character's shoes, drafting a screenplay late at night, diving across the badminton court on match point, or architecting a software module, the core driving force is identical: curating genuine emotion, discipline, and unforgettable moments.

🎬

The Stage & The Lens

Acting is my pursuit of raw human truth. Stepping into another soul's skin taught me deep empathy. Filmmaking naturally followed: crafting visual compositions where silence speaks louder than words.

🏸

The Court of Discipline

On the badminton court, everything condenses down to instinct, geometry, and relentless stamina. Badminton instilled in me mental composure under pressure, quick tactical shifts, and athletic tenacity.

💻 The Digital Canvas (B.Tech CSE Core)

As a 1st-year Computer Science Engineering student at JECRC University (Class of 2030), code to me isn't just lines in a terminal—it is an interactive medium of art. Building creative web experiences, retro canvas engines, and exploring algorithmic problem-solving allows engineering and imagination to walk side by side.
